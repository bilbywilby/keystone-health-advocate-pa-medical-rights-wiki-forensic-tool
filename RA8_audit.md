## 5. Findings

- **Competition.** 8 filers are listed (7 sell on Pennie). No county has two or fewer on-Pennie filers.
- **Price.** Carrier-mix approved increase +19.8%, +3.8 pts vs. the state average; rank 1 of 9. The spread between filers is 33.75 pts, so the filer you hold matters more than the area average.
- **PID action.** PID trimmed 4 of 7 requests here. It approved above the request for Keystone Health Plan East (+1.30 pts; PID's stated reason: worsening medical and drug trends); Health Partners Plans (+2.45 pts; PID's stated reason: less healthy membership confirmed through the federal risk adjustment program); QCC Insurance Co. (+1.43 pts; PID's stated reason: worsening medical and drug trends) (PID, 2026e).
- **Off-Pennie.** Ambetter Health Solutions (new) are not sold on Pennie, so premium tax credits cannot be applied to them.
- **Two-year view.** Largest compounded 2026-27 increase in the area: Ambetter Health of PA +86.3%; lowest: Partners Insurance Co. +6.8%. Gross premium, before tax credits.
- **Terminations.** No county is on Pennie's top-20 list; all are at or below the 19% floor.

## 6. Data quality and open items

- Carrier-mix is a proxy: statewide market shares weight each area because area enrollment is not published. The weights reproduce PID's statewide averages.
- Terminations are the published top 20 only; other counties are at or below 19%.
- Highmark Coverage Advantage filed individual rates for 2026 but not for 2027; where its enrollees moved is not confirmed. [UNVERIFIED]
