## 5. Findings

- **Competition.** 5 filers are listed (4 sell on Pennie). Two or fewer on-Pennie filers in 7 of 8 counties: Crawford, Erie, Forest, McKean, Mercer, Venango, Warren. These households have little choice if a carrier raises prices.
- **Price.** Carrier-mix approved increase +15.3%, -0.7 pts vs. the state average; rank 6 of 9. The spread between filers is 25.40 pts, so the filer you hold matters more than the area average.
- **PID action.** PID trimmed 4 of 4 requests here.
- **Off-Pennie.** Ambetter Health Solutions (new) are not sold on Pennie, so premium tax credits cannot be applied to them.
- **Two-year view.** Largest compounded 2026-27 increase in the area: Ambetter Health of PA +86.3%; lowest: UPMC Health Network +31.9%. Gross premium, before tax credits.
- **Terminations.** No county is on Pennie's top-20 list; all are at or below the 19% floor.

## 6. Data quality and open items

- Carrier-mix is a proxy: statewide market shares weight each area because area enrollment is not published. The weights reproduce PID's statewide averages.
- Terminations are the published top 20 only; other counties are at or below 19%.
- Highmark Coverage Advantage filed individual rates for 2026 but not for 2027; where its enrollees moved is not confirmed. [UNVERIFIED]
- This is one of the two thin-competition areas; see the county table.
