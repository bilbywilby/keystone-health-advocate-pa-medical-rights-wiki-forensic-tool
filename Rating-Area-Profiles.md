**What separates areas:** competition more than price spread. Areas 1 and 4 are thin (7 of 8 and 8 of 10 counties have two or fewer on-Pennie filers). Area 8 has the highest carrier-mix increase (+19.8%); Areas 2, 5 and 9 the lowest. Every area's approved range is wide, topped by Ambetter wherever it is listed.
