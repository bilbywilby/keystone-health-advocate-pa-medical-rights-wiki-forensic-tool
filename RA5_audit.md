## 5. Findings

- **Competition.** 7 filers are listed (6 sell on Pennie). No county has two or fewer on-Pennie filers.
- **Price.** Carrier-mix approved increase +14.8%, -1.2 pts vs. the state average; rank 9 of 9. The spread between filers is 26.56 pts, so the filer you hold matters more than the area average.
- **PID action.** PID trimmed 6 of 6 requests here.
- **Off-Pennie.** Ambetter Health Solutions (new) are not sold on Pennie, so premium tax credits cannot be applied to them.
- **Two-year view.** Largest compounded 2026-27 increase in the area: Ambetter Health of PA +86.3%; lowest: Geisinger Health Plan +21.2%. Gross premium, before tax credits.
- **Terminations.** Blair 19%, Clearfield 19% are on Pennie's top-20 list (through 2/1/2026).

## 6. Data quality and open items

- Carrier-mix is a proxy: statewide market shares weight each area because area enrollment is not published. The weights reproduce PID's statewide averages.
- Terminations are the published top 20 only; other counties are at or below 19%.
- Highmark Coverage Advantage filed individual rates for 2026 but not for 2027; where its enrollees moved is not confirmed. [UNVERIFIED]
